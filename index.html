Data & Privacy Notice

Effective date: 7 October 2026

Who we are

ChaceJoy is an Amazon store run by guangzhoushichaisikejiyouxiangongsi ("ChaceJoy", "we"). We use the Amazon Ads API through a small in-house program that only our own staff use. The program is not a product or service, and nobody outside our company can sign in to it or receive its output.

Information the program handles

The program reads advertising information for the ChaceJoy store only, namely:

  1. the structure and settings of our Sponsored Products and other ad campaigns;
  2. delivery and cost figures such as impressions, clicks, spend and attributed sales;
  3. reports showing which shopper search terms triggered our ads.

None of this identifies an individual shopper. The program never asks Amazon for buyer names, addresses, contact details or any other personal information.

Why we use it

We use the information to see how our advertising is performing and to decide on budgets, bids and keywords for our own campaigns. We do not use it for profiling, resale or marketing to third parties.

Where it is kept and for how long

Reports are saved on computers owned and controlled by our company. Login keys for the API are kept in a protected file, separate from any shared folder or code history. We delete reports once they are no longer needed for our own analysis.

Who sees it

Only authorised ChaceJoy staff. We do not pass, sell or lend this information to any other company, and we do not send it to outside analytics or advertising services.

Keeping it safe

Access to the API keys is limited to the people who need it. If we ever suspect a key has been exposed, we revoke and replace it. Wherever possible the program uses read-only access.

Amazon's rules

Our use of the API follows the Amazon Ads API License Agreement and Amazon's data protection requirements for advertising data.

Updates and contact

Any change to this notice will be posted on this page with a new effective date. For questions, write to chacetoy@163.com.
